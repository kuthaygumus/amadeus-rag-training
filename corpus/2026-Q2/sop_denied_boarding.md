# HELIOS AIR — STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
## SOP-DENIED-BOARDING — Oversale and Boarding Refusal

Document ID: OPS-SOP-DB
Version: 4 | Current
Effective: 2026-Q2
Issued by: Operations Control Centre (OCC), Istanbul (IST)
Applies to: All stations, Duty Managers, Gate Agents, Call Centre Tier 2

### 1. PURPOSE
This procedure governs the handling of a passenger holding a confirmed reservation who is
not carried on the flight for which that reservation is held, whether by oversale, weight
restriction, equipment substitution or downgrade.

### 2. VOLUNTEERS FIRST
2.1 Before any involuntary action, call for volunteers at the gate. Announce the routing
    of the alternative service and the benefit offered before taking names.
2.2 A volunteer who accepts the offer is recorded as VDB. Duty of care applies from the
    moment the original service departs. Compensation tiers in Section 4 do not apply to
    a VDB; the negotiated benefit replaces them.
2.3 If insufficient volunteers come forward, proceed to Section 3.

### 3. BOARDING PRIORITY (INVOLUNTARY)
Where boarding must be refused, the last passengers to be offloaded are, in order:
3.1 Unaccompanied minors, passengers with reduced mobility and their companions.
3.2 Passengers whose journey continues on an interline or codeshare segment the same day.
3.3 Passengers holding FLEX, then CLASSIC, then LITE.
3.4 Within a fare family, order by check-in time, earliest retained.

### 4. COMPENSATION TIERS (INVOLUNTARY DENIED BOARDING)
| Route band | Delay to final destination | Compensation per passenger |
|------------|----------------------------|----------------------------|
| SHORT-HAUL (Europe) | up to 2 hours   | EUR 125 |
| SHORT-HAUL (Europe) | over 2 hours    | EUR 250 |
| LONG-HAUL           | up to 4 hours   | EUR 300 |
| LONG-HAUL           | over 4 hours    | EUR 600 |

4.1 Compensation is paid in addition to, and never in place of, reaccommodation.
4.2 A downgrade is compensated at the reimbursement percentage of the sector fare set in
    the applicable fare rule sheet; do not quote a percentage from memory at the gate.

### 5. DUTY OF CARE
5.1 Meal voucher where the wait for the reaccommodated service exceeds 3 hours.
5.2 Hotel accommodation and surface transfer where the wait exceeds 6 hours, or where the
    reaccommodated service departs the following calendar day.
5.3 Two telephone calls or equivalent messaging, on request.
5.4 Duty of care is provided regardless of fare family. Duty of care is provided
    regardless of fare family, and is not deducted from compensation.

### 6. RECORDING
Annotate the PNR with the DB reason code, offload sequence and the agent identifier.
Send the station report to OCC before the end of shift. Late reports are querried by
Revenue Integrity at month end.
