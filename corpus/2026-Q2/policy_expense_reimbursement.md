# KRAKEN AIR — TRAVEL EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
## Finance Shared Services Centre / Accounts Payable

Policy number: **HR-TRV-06**
Version: 5.3 — 2026-Q2 issue
Effective: 2026-04-01
Policy owner: Head of Financial Control
Operational owner: Manager, Finance Shared Services Centre (FSSC), IST
Applies to: all employees claiming reimbursement of travel-related expenditure
Read with: HR-TRV-04 (Corporate Travel), HR-TRV-05 (Travel Authorisation)

---

### 1. WHAT THIS POLICY DOES

1.1 This policy states what an employee may claim back from the Company after travelling, the
evidence required, and the timescale. It is a **finance** policy addressed to the employee. It
has no application to a fare-paying passenger and the amounts stated here are not customer
entitlements.

1.2 Claims are submitted in the expense system within **30 calendar days** of the end of the
trip. Claims older than 60 days are declined by the FSSC and require a Band 7 exception to be
paid at all.

### 2. PER DIEM AND SUBSISTENCE

2.1 Subsistence is paid on a **per diem** basis, not on receipts, for trips involving an
overnight stay:

| Destination band | Per diem (24h) | Part day over 6h |
|---|---|---|
| Türkiye, domestic | EUR 45 | EUR 20 |
| Europe, short-haul | EUR 70 | EUR 30 |
| Long-haul | EUR 95 | EUR 40 |

2.2 The per diem covers meals, non-alcoholic drinks and incidentals. Receipts are not
required and are not to be attached. Where a meal is provided by the Company, by a conference
or by a hotel rate, the per diem is reduced by EUR 15 for that meal.

2.3 Alcohol is not reimbursable in any circumstance, including where it forms part of an
otherwise reimbursable meal. The claimant is to deduct it from the bill total.

### 3. HOTEL AND GROUND TRANSPORT

3.1 Hotels are booked through the CTD against the negotiated rate programme. Where no
negotiated property is available, the ceilings are EUR 140 per night in Europe and EUR 190 per
night long-haul, inclusive of tax and breakfast.

3.2 Ground transport is reimbursed at cost for public transport, airport transfer and standard
taxi. Ride-hailing is reimbursable at the standard tier. Premium and executive tiers are not
reimbursable and the difference is borne by the claimant.

3.3 Private car mileage is reimbursed at the rate published annually by Financial Control and
requires the journey's start and end points on the claim.

### 4. EXPENDITURE DURING A DISRUPTION

4.1 An employee whose Kraken or partner service is delayed, cancelled or misconnected while
travelling on Company business may claim **actual reasonable expenditure** incurred during the
disruption, against receipts, as follows:

| Item | Ceiling | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Meals and refreshments | **EUR 25 per meal**, maximum two meals in any 24 hours | Any delay exceeding **2 hours** |
| Hotel, where an overnight becomes unavoidable | Band ceilings at 3.1 | Any wait exceeding **4 hours** with no onward service |
| Ground transport to and from an unplanned hotel | At cost | With the hotel claim |
| Communications | EUR 15 per disruption | Receipt or itemised bill |

4.2 The ceilings at 4.1 are **reimbursement ceilings for employees** and are claimed after the
event through the expense system. They are set by Financial Control and are reviewed with this
policy each quarter.

4.3 Where the employee has already been given a voucher, a meal, or accommodation by the
handling station, the value received is deducted from the claim and the voucher reference is
entered on the claim line. Double recovery is treated as a claim irregularity under section 7.

4.4 An employee who is delayed more than four hours may use a contract lounge where one is
available, up to EUR 40, in place of the meal claim at 4.1 but not in addition to it.

### 5. UNUSED AND FORFEITED TICKETS

5.1 Where a trip is cancelled through the CTD before departure, no charge falls to the
traveller and the unused value is credited to the corporate account (HR-TRV-04 section 5.1).
**No cancellation penalty is charged to the employee or to the cost centre.**

5.2 Where the traveller no-shows without cancelling, the FSSC will nevertheless re-credit the
ticket value to the cost centre on the first occasion in any twelve-month period, on the
cost centre holder's confirmation that the absence was unavoidable. A second no-show in the
same period is charged to the cost centre in full.

5.3 Travellers are reminded that the no-show treatment above is a **Company recharge
arrangement between the traveller, the cost centre and the FSSC**. It has no bearing on how
the underlying ticket is treated under the applicable fare rules.

### 6. NON-REIMBURSABLE ITEMS

6.1 The following are never reimbursed: alcohol; minibar; in-flight duty free; fines and
penalty charges including parking and speeding; personal entertainment; laundry on trips
shorter than five nights; travel insurance purchased separately (the Company holds a
corporate policy); loyalty programme fees; upgrades purchased by the traveller.

### 7. EVIDENCE, AUDIT AND IRREGULARITY

7.1 Receipts are required for every line except per diem. A card statement is not a receipt.
7.2 The FSSC samples 10% of claims monthly and 100% of claims from any claimant with a prior
finding. 7.3 A claim found to be irregular is referred to the line manager and, where the
irregularity appears deliberate, to Internal Audit and to People &amp; Workplace.
