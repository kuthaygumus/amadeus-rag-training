# KRAKEN AIR — CONTACT CENTRE FAQ (GENERAL)

**Q1. My bag did not arrive. What happens now?**
A. A **PIR** (Property Irregularity Report) is raised and the passenger is given a **tracking number**. The bag is traced on that number. If it is not found within **21 days** it is declared **lost** and the compensation file is opened. Never raise a second PIR for the same bag; use the existing number.

**Q2. Which fare families does Kraken Air sell?**
A. **LITE**, **CLASSIC** and **FLEX**, on two route bands: SHORT-HAUL (Europe) and LONG-HAUL. LITE is the most restricted: cancellation is not permitted. Penalty amounts sit in the fare rule documents, per booking class and per direction. Agents read the amount from the rule, not from this FAQ.

**Q3. Kraken Air cancelled my flight. Do I pay to move to another flight?**
A. No. On an **airline-caused** cancellation a CLASSIC passenger pays **no** change penalty and is moved free of charge to the next Kraken service the same day. Penalties apply only to a **voluntary** cancellation or change.

**Q4. I did not show up for my flight. Can I still use the ticket?**
A. A no-show forfeits the change waiver and **doubles** the cancellation penalty stated in the fare rule. Remaining coupons are suspended until the amount is collected.

**Q5. I missed my connection at the hub. What am I entitled to?**
A. Duty of care depends on how long you wait for the next service. Meal vouchers and hotel accommodation are governed by **SOP-MISCONNECT**. Use the **current** revision — older revisions remain in the archive and carry different figures. Where the onward segment is operated by Wyvern Overseas (YY), the interline protection clause applies and the segment is not repriced.

**Q6. My flight time changed. Where do I check?**
A. Schedule change bulletins carry an SCB reference, for example **SCB-2026-0914** for **XX 1487** IST-CDG and **SCB-2026-0902** for **XX 2201** IST-FCO. A later bulletin supersedes an earlier one for the same flight, so confirm you are reading the latest reference.

**Q7. Are refunds and changes handled by the same team?**
A. No, they are seperate queues. Changes are done on the call; refunds are queued to ticketing and settle to the original form of payment in 7-14 days.
