# KRAKEN AIR — CORPORATE TRAVEL POLICY
## People &amp; Workplace / Finance Shared Services

Policy number: **HR-TRV-04**
Version: 6.1 — 2026-Q2 issue
Effective: 2026-04-01
Policy owner: Director, Total Rewards &amp; Mobility
Co-owner: Head of Financial Control (travel spend)
Applies to: all Kraken Air employees, fixed-term staff, and contractors engaged through a
Kraken purchase order who travel at Kraken expense
Review cycle: quarterly, with the fare-family reissue

---

### 1. PURPOSE AND PRINCIPLE

1.1 Kraken Air funds travel that is necessary to deliver the business plan. The governing
principle is **lowest logical fare, booked as early as reasonably possible, through the
approved channel**. This policy sets out what may be booked, in which class, and what the
traveller may do to a booking after it is made.

1.2 This is an HR policy. It states the entitlement of the employee against the Company. It
does not create any entitlement for a fare-paying customer and must not be quoted to one.

### 2. APPROVED BOOKING CHANNEL

2.1 All staff travel is booked through the **Corporate Travel Desk (CTD)**, reachable on
extension 4400 or at ctd@krakenair.example, or through the self-booking tool where the
traveller has been provisioned. Bookings made directly with a retail website, a third-party
agency or an airport ticket office are **out of policy** and the Finance Shared Services
Centre will decline the expense claim under HR-TRV-06.

2.2 Every booking must carry a valid **cost centre** and a **project code**. The cost centre
is that of the traveller's home department unless the travel is chargeable to a project, in
which case the project's cost centre applies and the project manager's approval is required
under HR-TRV-05.

### 3. CABIN AND FARE FAMILY ENTITLEMENT

3.1 Entitlement by grade and route band:

| Grade band | Short-haul (Europe) | Long-haul |
|---|---|---|
| Bands 1–4 (staff, specialist) | Economy, CLASSIC corporate fare | Economy, CLASSIC corporate fare |
| Bands 5–6 (manager, senior manager) | Economy, CLASSIC corporate fare | Economy, FLEX corporate fare |
| Bands 7+ (director and above) | Economy, FLEX corporate fare | Business, where flight time exceeds 7 hours |

3.2 The **LITE** fare family is not to be used for staff travel in any grade band. LITE fares
do not permit change and are not refundable, which is incompatible with the operational
flexibility this policy assumes. The CTD will not issue a LITE fare against a Kraken cost
centre.

3.3 Corporate CLASSIC fares are held in the standard CLASSIC booking classes — **O**, **T**,
**Q**, **K** and **M** — under the Kraken corporate account code. The class allocated by the
CTD is the lowest available in that set at the moment of booking. Travellers must not request
a specific booking class.

### 4. CHANGES TO A BOOKING

4.1 **Same-day changes are free of charge.** An employee travelling on a Kraken corporate
fare may change to any earlier or later Kraken service on the **same calendar day** as the
originally ticketed departure at **no charge to the traveller and no charge to the cost
centre**, in any of the CLASSIC booking classes O, T, Q, K or M, and irrespective of the
reason for the change. The change is made by calling the CTD or, within four hours of
departure, at the airport service desk on production of a staff identity card.

4.2 A change to a **different calendar day** requires the approval of the cost centre holder
and is charged to the cost centre at the difference in fare only. No change penalty is levied
on a corporate fare.

4.3 Where the traveller is on a **FLEX** corporate fare, changes are free of charge on any
date, consistent with the fare family.

4.4 The CTD is authorised to effect the changes described in 4.1 to 4.3 without further
reference. Airport staff handling a same-day change under 4.1 should annotate the record with
the staff number and the cost centre and take no payment.

### 5. CANCELLATION AND UNUSED TICKETS

5.1 A trip that is no longer required must be cancelled through the CTD **before departure**.
A corporate fare cancelled before departure is returned to the corporate account as an unused
value credit, valid twelve months, and the cost centre is credited in the following month's
recharge.

5.2 Travellers must not simply fail to travel. A no-show consumes the ticket value and
generates an exception report to the cost centre holder and to Financial Control.

### 6. ANCILLARIES

6.1 Seat selection is reimbursable up to EUR 20 per sector on flights over three hours and is
not reimbursable below that. 6.2 One checked bag is within policy on short-haul; two on
long-haul. 6.3 Lounge access is not reimbursable except for Bands 7+ and except where the
traveller is delayed more than four hours, in which case see HR-TRV-06 section 4.4.
