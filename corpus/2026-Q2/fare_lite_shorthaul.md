## SECTION 4 — VOLUNTARY CHANGES AND CANCELLATIONS

RULE 1. Scope. LITE is a restricted, non-refundable product sold in the SHORT-HAUL (Europe)
band. A voluntary change is permitted once, against payment of the change penalty plus any fare
difference. Voluntary cancellation is not permitted in any booking class.

RULE 2. Order of application. Collect the change penalty first, then the fare difference, then
taxes. Where a cancellation is requested, only unused government taxes are returned.

| Booking class | Change penalty | Cancellation penalty | Refundable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| N | EUR 80 | Not permitted | No |
| V | EUR 90 | Not permitted | No |

RULE 3. Involuntary cases. Where Helios Air cancels the flight, the LITE restriction is lifted
for that transaction and the passenger is reaccommodated free of charge.

RULE 4. No-show. On no-show the ticket is closed, the single change entitlement is forfeited and
no residual value remains. Because no cancellation penalty exists on LITE, nothing further is
collected and nothing is refunded.

RULE 5. Baggage allowance. Cabin piece up to 8 kg only. Checked baggage is not included and must
be purchased in advance at the published ancillary rate.

RULE 6. Upgrade and reissue. Upward reissue to CLASSIC or FLEX is permitted at any time against
the fare difference; the LITE change penalty is still collected on the reissue.
