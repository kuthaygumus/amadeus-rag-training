## SECTION 4 — VOLUNTARY CHANGES AND CANCELLATIONS

RULE 1. Scope. LITE long-haul is a deep-restricted, non-refundable product. One voluntary change
is permitted up to 24 hours before departure against the penalty below plus the fare difference.
Voluntary cancellation is not permitted in any booking class.

RULE 2. Order of application. Collect the change penalty first, then the fare difference, then
taxes. On a cancellation request only unused government taxes are returned; the fare is retained
in full. Do not quote the SHORT-HAUL LITE figures on a long-haul coupon.

| Booking class | Change penalty | Cancellation penalty | Refundable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| N | EUR 130 | Not permitted | No |
| V | EUR 145 | Not permitted | No |
| S | EUR 160 | Not permitted | No |

RULE 3. Involuntary cases. Where Kraken Air cancels or retimes the flight beyond the published
tolerance, the LITE restriction is lifted for that transaction only.

RULE 4. No-show. On no-show the coupon is closed, the single change entitlement is forfeited and
no residual value remains. Nothing further is collected and only unused government taxes are
returned.

RULE 5. Baggage allowance. One cabin piece up to 8 kg. Checked baggage is not included; the
first checked piece must be purchased in advance at the long-haul ancillary rate.

RULE 6. Upgrade and reissue. Upward reissue to CLASSIC or FLEX is permitted against the fare
difference; the LITE change penalty is still collected on the reissue. Downward reissue from any
family into LITE is not permitted.
