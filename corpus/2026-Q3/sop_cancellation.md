# KRAKEN AIR — STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
## SOP-CANCELLATION — Flight Cancellation and Passenger Reaccommodation

Document ID: OPS-SOP-CXL
Version: 4 | Current
Effective: 2026-Q3
Issued by: Operations Control Centre (OCC), Istanbul (IST)
Applies to: All stations, Duty Managers, Hub Control, Call Centre Tier 2

### 1. PURPOSE
This procedure governs the handling of passengers on a Kraken service cancelled by the
carrier, for any reason, including cancellations decided within 14 days of departure and
those decided on the day of operation.

### 2. CLASSIFICATION
2.1 An **airline-caused** cancellation is any cancellation not requested by the passenger,
    including commercial, technical and crew causes.
2.2 An **extraordinary circumstance** cancellation (weather, air traffic management, civil
    unrest, security event) removes the compensation entitlement in Section 5 but does not
    remove duty of care in Section 4 or the reaccommodation duty in Section 3.
2.3 A cancellation requested by the passenger is a **voluntary** cancellation and is not
    handled here. See Section 6.

### 3. REBOOKING PRIORITY
3.1 Next available Kraken service to the ticketed destination, same day where possible.
3.2 Kraken service to an alternative airport in the same city or catchment, with surface
    transfer at Kraken cost.
3.3 Interline or codeshare partner service, where an agreement is in force for the sector.
3.4 Within each option, protect connecting passengers before point-to-point passengers,
    then order by original check-in status.
3.5 Reaccommodation under this section is at no charge to the passenger. No change fee,
    fare difference or reissue penalty is collected on an airline-caused cancellation.

### 4. DUTY OF CARE THRESHOLDS
| Wait for reaccommodated service | Provision |
|---------------------------------|-----------|
| over 2 hours | Refreshment |
| over 3 hours | Meal voucher |
| over 6 hours | Hotel accommodation and surface transfer |

Duty of care is provided regardless of fare family and regardless of cause.

### 5. COMPENSATION
5.1 Compensation is assessed by the Customer Relations desk, not at station level.
5.2 Where the passenger is offered reaccommodation departing within 2 hours of the
    original schedule, the compensation assessment records a mitigated tier.
5.3 Section 2.2 cases are recorded with the cause code and closed without compensation.

### 6. VOLUNTARY CANCELLATION AND REFUND
6.1 Where the passenger declines the reaccommodation offered under Section 3 and asks to
    cancel, the case leaves this procedure.
6.2 Determine the fare family and booking class from the fare basis in the ticket, then
    apply the change penalty, cancellation penalty and refundability stated in the fare
    rule sheet for that fare family and route band for the current quarter. Do not quote
    a penalty amount from this procedure, from a superseded quarter, or from memory.
6.3 Where the passenger did not travel and did not cancel before departure, the no-show
    treatment in the fare rule sheet applies instead.
