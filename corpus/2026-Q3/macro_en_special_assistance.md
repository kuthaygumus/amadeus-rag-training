# MACRO: SPCL-EN-042 — Special Assistance (WCHR / UMNR / Medical)

Document: CRM-MACRO-EN-042 | Revision: 3 | Effective: 2026-Q3 | Channel: Contact Centre (EN)
Escalation: SPCLSVC-IST | SLA: 4 hours

**Q: A passenger needs wheelchair assistance at IST and at the destination. How is it recorded?**

A: Add the correct SSR to the PNR at least 48 hours before departure. Use WCHR, WCHS or WCHC according to mobility, and repeat the SSR on **every** segment, including any interline segment operated by Wyvern Overseas. An SSR on only the first segment will not travel with the passenger.

**Step 1:** Confirm the SSR code with the passenger before entering it.
**Step 2:** Enter the SSR on each coupon. For an YY-operated segment, send the request to the partner and wait for the KK confirmation before telling the passenger it is confirmed.
**Step 3:** For UMNR, collect the guardian contact at both ends.
**Step 4:** For medical cases requiring oxygen or a stretcher, do not confirm on the call. Raise the medical clearance form and advise that the passenger will recieve a decision within 48 hours.

**Q: The passenger misconnected and needs assistance while waiting. Who pays?**

A: Assistance during a disruption is handled under the disruption procedure, not this macro. Duty-of-care entitlements such as meal vouchers and hotel accommodation are defined in the current SOP-MISCONNECT only. Airport staff arrange transfer once the entitlement is confirmed.

**Q: Special assistance was requested but not delivered. What now?**

A: Log a service failure report with the flight number, date, SSR code. Compensation is assessed by the service recovery desk; agents do not commit amounts on the call.
