# KRAKEN AIR — TRAVEL AUTHORISATION AND APPROVAL POLICY
## Finance Shared Services / Procurement Governance

Policy number: **HR-TRV-05**
Version: 4.8 — 2026-Q3 issue
Effective: 2026-07-01
Policy owner: Head of Financial Control
Delegated authority framework: DOA-2026 (Delegation of Authority Schedule, rev. 3)
Applies to: all travel requests raised against a Kraken Air cost centre
Read with: HR-TRV-04 (Corporate Travel Policy), HR-TRV-06 (Expense Reimbursement)

---

### 1. AUTHORISATION IS PRIOR, NOT RETROSPECTIVE

1.1 No travel may be booked before it is authorised. A travel request approved after the
ticket has been issued is a **policy breach** and is recorded as such against the approving
manager's cost centre, whether or not the expense is ultimately met.

1.2 Retrospective authorisation may be granted only by a Band 7 approver and only in the
circumstances at section 5 (operational recall and disruption recovery).

### 2. THE APPROVAL CHAIN

2.1 Approvals flow through the Travel Request (TR) workflow in the finance system. The chain
is:

| Trip value (total, all sectors and ancillaries) | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to EUR 750 | Line manager (Band 5+) |
| EUR 751 – EUR 3,000 | Cost centre holder |
| EUR 3,001 – EUR 10,000 | Department head plus Financial Control review |
| Above EUR 10,000 | Executive Committee member, countersigned by the CFO |

2.2 Where travel is chargeable to a project, the project manager approves **in addition to**,
not instead of, the cost centre holder. Where a traveller approves their own request the TR
is rejected automatically by the workflow; self-approval is not available at any band.

2.3 An approver who is absent must delegate in the system. Verbal delegation, email
delegation and "approved on his behalf" annotations are not accepted by Financial Control and
will be reversed at month-end recharge.

### 3. COST CENTRE AND RECHARGE MECHANICS

3.1 Every TR carries one cost centre and, where applicable, one project code. Split coding
across two cost centres is permitted only for genuinely shared trips and requires both cost
centre holders to approve.

3.2 Travel spend is recharged to the cost centre in the month the ticket is **issued**, not
the month it is flown. Cancellations and credits are recharged in the month the credit is
received. Cost centre holders reconcile the travel recharge line as part of the standard
month-end pack.

### 4. CHANGES AND CANCELLATIONS — AUTHORISATION TREATMENT

4.1 A **same-day** change made under HR-TRV-04 section 4.1 requires **no new TR and no
further approval**. It carries no charge to the cost centre and is therefore outside the
approval thresholds at section 2. The Corporate Travel Desk records it against the original
TR number.

4.2 A change to a different calendar day requires a TR amendment approved by the cost centre
holder. Only the fare difference is charged; **no change penalty and no cancellation penalty
is applied to a Kraken corporate fare cancelled or amended through the Corporate Travel
Desk**, in any booking class, on any route band.

4.3 Where the change is caused by the Company — a meeting moved, an operational recall, a
roster change — the change is authorised by exception and the cost centre holder is notified
rather than asked.

### 5. OPERATIONAL RECALL AND DISRUPTION RECOVERY

5.1 Where a traveller is recalled to duty, or is caught in a disruption to the Kraken network
and must be recovered, the Duty Manager (Operations Control, IST) has standing authority to
authorise travel to the value of EUR 5,000 without a prior TR. The TR is raised within
three working days and is approved retrospectively under 1.2.

5.2 Standing authority under 5.1 covers carriage, hotel accommodation and ground transport.
It does not cover entertainment, alcohol or upgrades, which remain out of policy in all
circumstances.

5.3 Where the traveller is recovered on an interline or partner service, the CTD raises the
reaccommodation with the partner desk. Staff must not purchase a new ticket on a partner
carrier at the airport while a recovery is in progress; a duplicate purchase is recharged to
the traveller's cost centre in full.

### 6. BREACH AND ESCALATION

6.1 Out-of-policy bookings are reported monthly to the Executive Committee by cost centre.
6.2 A first breach is addressed by the line manager. 6.3 A repeated pattern of breach is
handled under the Company's disciplinary procedure. 6.4 Deliberate splitting of a trip to
fall below an approval threshold is treated as a financial control failure and is escalated
directly to Internal Audit.
