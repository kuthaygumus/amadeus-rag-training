# KRAKEN AIR — STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
## SOP-MISCONNECT — Passenger Misconnection at a Kraken Hub or Online Station

Document ID: OPS-SOP-MISCONNECT
Version: 4 | Current
Effective: 2026-Q3
Issued by: Operations Control Centre (OCC), Istanbul (IST)
Applies to: All stations, Duty Managers, Hub Control, Call Centre Tier 2

### 1. PURPOSE
This procedure defines the handling of a passenger who fails to make an onward connection
where the inbound Kraken service arrived late, or where a schedule change reduced the
connecting time below the published minimum connect time (MCT).

### 2. SCOPE
Applies to all Kraken Air (XX) marketed and operated services, both SHORT-HAUL (Europe)
and LONG-HAUL route bands, and to itineraries containing an interline segment issued on a
Kraken document. Voluntary changes made by the passenger are out of scope and are handled
under the applicable fare rule sheet.

### 3. PROCEDURE
3.1 Confirm the misconnect in the departure control system and annotate the PNR with the
    inbound flight number and actual arrival time.
3.2 Identify the next available service to the ticketed destination. Rebook the passenger
    on the next available Kraken service **or interline partner service**, whichever
    departs first, subject to 3.6. Do not reprice the itinerary.
3.3 Establish the connection wait, measured from the actual arrival time of the inbound
    service to the scheduled departure of the reaccommodated service.
3.4 Where the connection wait exceeds **3 hours**, issue a meal voucher to the value of
    **EUR 15** per passenger. Infants without a seat are not entitled to a voucher.
3.5 Where the connection wait exceeds **6 hours**, arrange hotel accommodation with
    surface transfer. Hotel is arranged only through the contracted station provider.
3.6 Where the onward segment is operated by Wyvern Overseas (YY), the reaccommodation is
    governed by **Clause 4 — Protection on Irregular Operations** of the XX/YY interline
    agreement. Apply that clause as written; do not improvise a settlement at station level.
3.7 Where a passenger elects not to travel at all, do not process the case here. The
    applicable fare rule sheet for the fare family and booking class governs.
3.8 Record the disruption reference in the OCC daily log within 24 hours.

### 4. DUTY OF CARE
Duty of care is provided regardless of fare family. This procedure establishes no monetary
penalty, waiver or refund value of any kind; all such amounts are held in the fare rule
sheets for the relevant quarter and are quoted from there only.
