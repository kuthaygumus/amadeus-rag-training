## SECTION 4 — VOLUNTARY CHANGES AND CANCELLATIONS

RULE 1. Scope. FLEX is the fully flexible short-haul product. Changes are permitted without
penalty in classes Y and B at any time before or after departure, and in class H against the
nominal penalty in the table below, subject to fare difference and availability in the original
or a higher booking class.

RULE 2. Order of application. Where a penalty is shown, collect it first, then the fare
difference, then taxes. Refunds are processed to the original form of payment within 7 working
days and no service fee is added on a direct-channel refund.

| Booking class | Change penalty | Cancellation penalty | Refundable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Y | EUR 0 | EUR 0 | Yes |
| B | EUR 0 | EUR 25 | Yes |
| H | EUR 20 | EUR 35 | Yes |

RULE 3. Involuntary cases. Involuntary reaccommodation is always free of charge on FLEX and does
not consume any change entitlement.

RULE 4. No-show. Where the passenger does not present for carriage, the change waiver is
forfeited and the cancellation penalty is doubled before any refund is computed. In class Y,
where the penalty is EUR 0, the doubled amount remains EUR 0 and the fare stays refundable.

RULE 5. Baggage allowance. Two checked pieces up to 23 kg each plus one cabin piece up to 8 kg,
with priority handling at IST.

RULE 6. Upgrade and reissue. Unlimited reissue within FLEX at no penalty. Downward reissue to
CLASSIC or LITE is permitted only before the first coupon is flown and the residual value is
refunded to the original form of payment.
