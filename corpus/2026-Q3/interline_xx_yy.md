### RECITALS

WHEREAS Kraken Air ("XX", the "First Party") operates a scheduled network from its hub at
Istanbul (IST) and Wyvern Overseas Airways ("YY", the "Second Party") operates a scheduled
network from its gateways at Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG), Amsterdam (AMS) and New York
John F. Kennedy (JFK); and WHEREAS the Parties wish to accept each other's traffic documents
on an interline basis pursuant to the Multilateral Interline Traffic Agreement — Passenger
(MITA-P) and the bilateral supplements attached hereto as Appendices A through F; NOW
THEREFORE the Parties agree as follows.

---

### CLAUSE 1 — SCOPE AND ACCEPTANCE OF TRAFFIC DOCUMENTS

1.1 Each Party shall accept for carriage the electronic traffic documents of the other Party
issued in accordance with Resolution 722f and validated against the issuing Party's stock,
provided the coupon status is OPEN FOR USE at the moment of acceptance and the itinerary
has been constructed within the interline construction points listed in Appendix A.

1.2 Acceptance under sub-clause 1.1 constitutes acceptance of the carriage obligation only.
It shall not be construed as acceptance of any commercial condition, service standard,
ancillary entitlement or compensatory obligation of the other Party, all of which remain
governed by the conditions of carriage of the Party whose designator appears against the
coupon in question.

1.3 Neither Party assumes any obligation to accept traffic documents presented outside the
interline construction points, and any such acceptance, if granted as a goodwill measure,
shall be treated as an isolated act and shall not establish a course of dealing.

### CLAUSE 2 — BAGGAGE THROUGH-CHECK AND ACCEPTANCE

2.1 Checked baggage shall be through-checked to the final ticketed destination on the
itinerary where the entire journey is held on a single traffic document or on conjunctive
documents issued at the same time, and where the connecting sectors fall within the minimum
connecting time published in the Official Airline Guide for the transfer airport.

2.2 The free baggage allowance applicable to the whole journey shall be that of the
Most Significant Carrier as determined under Resolution 302, notwithstanding that a lesser
allowance may be shown against an individual sector in the reservation record.

2.3 Where baggage is delayed, damaged or not recovered, the Party at whose station the
irregularity was reported shall open the file and shall act as the Party of first contact.
Settlement between the Parties shall be effected under the baggage settlement provisions of
Appendix D and shall not be discussed with the passenger by the reporting station.

2.4 Nothing in this Clause 2 shall be read as conferring on the passenger any entitlement
against either Party beyond that arising under the applicable convention and the conditions
of carriage of the carrier concerned.

### CLAUSE 3 — TICKETING AUTHORITY AND FARE CONSTRUCTION

3.1 Each Party grants the other the authority to issue against its stock for sectors it
operates, subject to the ticketing designator and the fare basis codes filed for the market
concerned. Such authority is limited to published fares, negotiated fares filed under an
agreed tour code, and interline-eligible private fares where expressly permitted.

3.2 The conditions attaching to a fare — including but not limited to advance purchase,
minimum and maximum stay, permitted stopovers, and the penalties applicable to voluntary
change, voluntary cancellation and no-show — shall be those of the carrier whose fare has
been used to construct the sector, irrespective of which Party issued the document.

3.3 A Party issuing against the other Party's stock shall not vary, waive, discount or
otherwise reinterpret a filed condition. Any waiver shall be requested in writing from the
owning Party's revenue management desk and shall be evidenced by an endorsement in the
traffic document.

### CLAUSE 4 — PROTECTION ON IRREGULAR OPERATIONS

4.1 Where a delay, cancellation, diversion, equipment substitution or other irregularity in
the operation of a Kraken Air service causes a passenger to misconnect onto an onward Wyvern
Overseas segment held on the same itinerary, **the Wyvern Overseas segment is protected**.
The Wyvern Overseas coupon shall not be treated as a no-show, shall not be cancelled by
schedule-driven queue action, and shall retain its OPEN FOR USE status pending
reaccommodation.

4.2 In the circumstances described in sub-clause 4.1, **Kraken Air may reaccommodate the
passenger on the next available Wyvern Overseas service without repricing**. No fare
recalculation, no collection of a difference in fare, no reissue fee and no change penalty
shall arise from a reaccommodation effected under this clause, and the original fare basis
shall be carried forward to the replacement coupon.

4.3 The protection conferred by this clause runs in the reciprocal direction on identical
terms where an Wyvern Overseas irregularity causes a misconnect onto a Kraken Air segment.

4.4 This clause establishes the entitlement to reaccommodation and the pricing treatment of
that reaccommodation. It does not establish, and shall not be read as establishing, the
ground-handling care owed to the passenger during the interval before departure of the
replacement service, the thresholds at which such care becomes due, or the monetary value of
any voucher, meal, refreshment or hotel accommodation. Those matters are dealt with
exclusively in the operating carrier's own standard operating procedure for misconnection,
as amended from time to time.

4.5 Nor does this clause determine the penalty consequences of a change requested by the
passenger for reasons unconnected with the irregularity. Such a request falls to be treated
as a voluntary change under Clause 3.2 and under the fare rule sheet governing the booking
class held.

### CLAUSE 5 — REVENUE PRORATION AND SETTLEMENT

5.1 Revenue on interline itineraries shall be prorated on the Straight Rate Proration basis
using the Prorate Factors published in the Multilateral Prorate Agreement, save for the
markets listed in Appendix C, which shall be settled on a Special Prorate Agreement basis at
the rates therein stated.

5.2 Where a coupon is flown under the protection conferred by Clause 4, the value proration
shall follow the original coupon value and not the value of the service actually flown.
Neither Party shall raise a rejection memo on the sole ground that the flown carrier differs
from the ticketed carrier where the reaccommodation was effected under Clause 4.

5.3 Settlement shall be through the IATA Clearing House on the standard monthly cycle.
Rejection memoranda shall be raised within the period stated in Appendix E and shall lapse
thereafter.

### CLAUSE 6 — LIABILITY, INDEMNITY AND LIMITATION

6.1 Each Party shall be liable to the passenger only in respect of the sectors it operates,
and the Montreal Convention 1999 limits shall apply as amended by periodic revision of the
Special Drawing Right thresholds.

6.2 Neither Party shall be liable to the other for indirect, consequential, punitive or
exemplary loss, nor for loss of profit, loss of goodwill or loss of anticipated saving,
howsoever arising, whether in contract, tort or otherwise.

6.3 Each Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other against any claim brought by a
passenger in respect of a sector operated by the indemnifying Party, save to the extent the
claim arises from the negligent act or omission of the indemnified Party.

6.4 The Parties shall each maintain aviation liability insurance in an amount not less than
that stated in Appendix F and shall furnish certificates of currency annually.

### CLAUSE 7 — TERM, VARIATION AND GOVERNING LAW

7.1 This Agreement shall continue in force until terminated by either Party on ninety (90)
days' written notice. 7.2 No variation shall be effective unless in writing and signed by an
authorised signatory of each Party. 7.3 This Agreement is governed by the law of England and
Wales and the Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of that
jurisdiction.
